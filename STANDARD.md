# Project rules

1. Keep README, PROCESS and STANDARD at the root; place screens, styles, content and tools in their dedicated directories.
2. Use English in the repository. Update matching Chinese documents in the local workspace alongside changed Current guides.
3. Keep one canonical owner per fact. The editor and game share schema, simulation and scene recipes; presentation never moves colliders.
4. Keep Current accurate, History dated and Plans explicit about unfinished work. Record player feedback, decisions, AI use and actual checks in PROCESS.
5. Preserve static GitHub Pages play, source attribution and proportionate regression checks. Keep private research and credentials outside Git.

6. Publish only character assets with recorded sharing permission; keep source, license and hashes in the catalogue.
